Step 1 · Ideation

Exploration of Graduate Pain Points

The "Interview Gap" in Vietnam

Vietnamese final-year students and fresh graduates face an intense employment market but lack the resources to practice mock interviews. Human coaching is expensive (cost-sensitive audience), and feedback is highly subjective.

Target users: Entry-level and Intern candidates in IT, Finance, and Business/Marketing.

Identified "Pain Points"

  • 1. Cost Obstacles: Traditional 1-on-1 expert mock interviews cost between 500,000 VND and 1,500,000 VND per hour.
  • 2. Lack of Standardization: Reviewers assess candidates on personal bias, rather than objective metrics.
  • 3. Low Practice Frequency: Candidates cannot afford to fail multiple live rounds simply to practice.
Step 1 · Ideation

Concept Inception & Narrative

Introducing Invera AI

Invera was conceptualized as a web application that replicates the pressure of actual interview questions. It uses advanced Large Language Models to evaluate candidates against structured metrics, outputting actionable advice in real time.

Narrative: "Practice unlimited, get expert-grade feedback, pay a fraction of the cost."

Step 3 · Insights

Core Survey Insights & Strategic Pivots

Strategic Insights

The survey data exposed a massive obstacle: while graduates found the mock concept useful, they hesitated to buy paid packages immediately. They also confused the app with a simple chatbot.

Pivot: Force high activation with a stunning Free Trial, and show visual tutorial/demo videos on landing.

Actions Taken Based on Data

  • 1. Pricing Adjustment: Lowered entry barrier by offering a lifetime free session, and per-session buy-add options (30k-35k VND).
  • 2. Chatbot vs. Platform: Redesigned UI to show structured analytics tables and charts, proving value beyond standard chats.
  • 3. Privacy Assurances: Drafted a formal Privacy Policy following VN Decree 13/2023 to secure resume file uploads.

Step 4 · Rubric

Standardizing the AI Rubric

Standardized Evaluation Prompts

To prevent AI scoring inconsistencies, CPO Nhi standardized prompt templates inside `BE/docs/AI_SCORING_RUBRIC.md`. AI is instructed to return feedback in a JSON payload.

LLM system prompt forces DeepSeek to output evidence quotes from user answers.

5 Rubric Criteria (Out of 10)

  • 1. Relevance: Direct answer to the question, avoiding off-topic filler sentences.
  • 2. Structure: Logic flow; ideally structured using the STAR method for behavioral questions.
  • 3. Logical Thinking: Argument coherence, quantitative references, trade-off analysis.
  • 4. Clarity: Professional grammar, concise sentences, communication style.
  • 5. Completeness: Addresses all parts of the question, including secondary requests.
Step 4 · Cost Controls

Heuristic Fallback & Cost Controls

Local Fallback Mechanisms

To protect business cash flow and ensure system reliability during API failures, the backend integrates local cost control checks and a keyword-matching scoring fallback.

Helps manage DeepSeek API quotas and mitigates network timeouts.

Implemented Logic

  • ✓ Keyword-matching fallback: Extracts tokens and checks overlap against the question ideal answer.
  • ✓ Gibberish Guard: Automatically assigns a low score (e.g. 0.4 - 2.5) if the answer is too short or contains gibberish patterns.
  • ✓ Standardized Prompts: System prompt limits verbose LLM responses, saving token costs.

Step 6 · GTM Execution

GTM: Online Payment Integrations

Revenue Activation Flow

To transition from a class project to a commercial business, we integrated payment gateways. The backend supports PayOS payment links and VNPay checkout redirects.

Webhook callbacks automatically activate paid plans in the database.

Order Lifecycle
